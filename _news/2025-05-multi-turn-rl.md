A new paper *Reinforcing Multi-Turn Reasoning in LLM Agents via Turn-Level Credit Assignment*, joint work with Siliang, Quan, William (Prime Intellect), Oana (Morgan Stanley), Yuriy Nevmyvaka (Morgan Stanley) is available [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11821). In this work, we show that it is critical to perform credit assignment when training LLMs for multi-turn agent applications. Code available [here](https://github.com/SiliangZeng/Multi-Turn-RL-Agent).
