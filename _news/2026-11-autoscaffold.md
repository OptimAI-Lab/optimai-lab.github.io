A new paper *AutoScaffold: AI-Guided Scaffolding for Reinforcement Learning of Language Models*, joint work with Zijian Zhang, Rizhen Hu, Jon, Shiyang Li, Sujay Bhatt and Sihan Zeng, is available [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14515). In this work, we let a frozen teacher model review the student's training attempts, identify where it gets stuck, and write training-time hints -- both strategies shared across task types and hints for individual hard tasks -- which are then removed at evaluation.
