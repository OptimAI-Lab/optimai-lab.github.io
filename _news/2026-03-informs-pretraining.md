Together with Dr. [Jiaxiang Li](https://jasonjiaxiangli.github.io/), M. organized two sessions on optimizer design and benchmarking for LLM pretraining at the **INFORMS Optimization Society Annual Meeting** (March 2026).

- **Session 1:** Kaan Ozkara (Amazon), Tuo Zhao (Georgia Tech), Meisam Razaviyayn (USC), Maria-Eleni Sfyraki (UC San Diego).
- **Session 2:** Weijie Su (Wharton, University of Pennsylvania), Athanasios Glentis Georgoulakis (University of Minnesota), Kaiyue Wen (Stanford University), Aaron Defazio (Meta), Hao-Jun Michael Shi (Meta).
