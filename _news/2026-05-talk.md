M. gave a talk at the [Department of Industrial Engineering and Management Sciences (IEMS)](https://www.mccormick.northwestern.edu/industrial/) at Northwestern University, titled "When Classical Optimization Meets Modern Foundation Models: New Algorithms, Theory, and Insights". The slides can be found [here](assets/pdf/Optimizer_Slides.pdf). This talk included a few of our new results, including an interpretation of [SGD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17787), and making Nesterov's lookahead momentum work as a "harness" to accelerate pretraining algorithms (see [here](https://oscaryau525.github.io/uploads/EMA_Nesterov.pdf)).
