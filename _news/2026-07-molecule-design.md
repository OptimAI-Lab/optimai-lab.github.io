A new paper *Generating Developable 3D Molecules via Pocket-Conditioned Diffusion and Property-Aware Optimization*, joint work with Jon and the group of Xia Ning (Ohio State University), is available [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12349). In this work, we study structure-based drug design, and propose a diffusion framework that generates ligands optimized jointly for predicted binding affinity and favorable ADMET properties.
