A new paper *Is One Layer Enough? Training A Single Transformer Layer Can Match Full-Parameter RL Training*, joint work with Zijian Zhang, Rizhen Hu, Athanasios, Dawei, Oscar and Hongzhou Lin, is available [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01232). In this work, we study how the gains of RL post-training are distributed across the layers of a transformer, and find that training a single well-chosen layer -- typically one in the middle of the network -- recovers most of what full-parameter RL achieves.
